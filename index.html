Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, daily routines, and school policies.

For Play School, children should be 2 to 3 years old by the time of admission. For LKG, children should have completed 3 years (3 to 4 years). For UKG, the recommended age is 4 to 5+ years. Slight grace periods are accommodated based on child readiness.
Our medium of instruction is English, supported by gentle bilingual guidance in Malayalam during the initial transition period so that children feel fully comfortable and secure while effortlessly acquiring English fluency.
Play School operates from 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM. LKG runs from 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM, and UKG operates from 9:30 AM to 2:30 PM (Monday through Friday). The administrative office is open from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM for parent visits.
Yes! We have safe, verified school vehicles covering Punnakkal and surrounding nearby towns and residential clusters with experienced drivers and accompanying female attendants.
Safety is our top priority. Our entire campus is monitored by high-definition CCTV cameras. All classroom furniture has rounded, child-safe edges, and our restrooms are regularly sanitized by full-time female caregivers.
