Curriculum & Learning Stages

A carefully structured progressive pathway crafted for Play School, LKG, and UKG students to transition smoothly into formal primary schooling.

Play School (Ages 2 – 3)

Timing: 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Foundational Stage

Play School: Nurturing Toddler Wonder

For many toddlers, Play School is their very first step into the world outside the warmth of home. Our priority is to make this transition gentle, tear-free, and full of exciting discoveries.

Sensory & Tactile Play: Water tables, sand play, kinetic squishing, and textured fabric touch mats.
Rhythm, Rhymes & Music: Daily musical circles with xylophones, shakers, and action rhymes.
Social & Emotional Milestones: Sharing toys, expressing basic needs politely, learning friendship habits.
Gentle Potty & Hygiene Routine: Experienced, patient caregivers assist with toilet training and handwashing.
Developmental Stage

LKG (Lower Kindergarten): Phonics & Expression

Lower Kindergarten (ages 3 to 4) is where cognitive building blocks take firm shape. Children learn letter sounds rather than mere letter names, unlocking early words naturally.

Jolly Phonics Methodology: Multi-sensory letter actions, sound cards, and audio associations.
Number Sense & Shapes: Concrete counting with beads, sorting blocks, recognizing patterns and sizes.
Pre-Writing & Fine Motor Skills: Sand tracing, clay modeling, crayon grips, and scissor control.
Spoken English Confidence: Daily show-and-tell, puppet dialogue, and question-answer routines.

LKG (Ages 3 – 4)

Timing: 9:30 AM – 1:30 PM

UKG (Ages 4 – 5+)

Timing: 9:30 AM – 2:30 PM

Readiness Stage

UKG (Upper Kindergarten): Primary School Readiness

In UKG (ages 4 to 5+), children develop true self-reliance, fluent sentence reading, simple addition/subtraction concepts, and stage presence that makes Grade 1 entry effortless and joyful.

Early Reading & Blending: Three-letter CVC words, sight words, short storybook reading.
Foundational Mathematics: Addition concepts using real objects, backward counting, time & calendar awareness.
General Knowledge & Nature Science: Plant life cycles, animal habitats, weather, community helpers.
Stage Presence & Elocution: Recitation, role-play dialogues, Annual Day cultural stage performances.
